# Roadmap from 2026-10-21

Planned work from 2026-10-21 on. It replaces
[`ROADMAP_2026-10-06.md`](ROADMAP_2026-10-06.md), which is archived as the record
of the work before: the restructure into one eDNA, one visual and one joint
model (Phases 0–5), the move to the 35°N study domain (6.0), the model changes
tried and adopted or not (6.2), and their results. Results go in the README and
the explainer (`docs/explainer.md`); this file lists what is still to do.

## 1. Goal: show the model works at three levels

1. **Idealised simulation** (*in progress*, section 3). The truth is drawn from
   the model's own structure (a GP plus a bottom-depth spline), observed at 200
   uniform eDNA stations and 35 evenly spaced east–west transects across the
   study domain.
2. **Realistic simulation** (*planned*, section 4). The truth is previously
   modelled density surfaces for PWSD, humpback and hake, observed at the
   **actual** ship transects and eDNA stations. The question is how well the
   model recovers the "true" surfaces under the real sampling design, when the
   truth is not a draw from the model.
3. **Real data** (*in progress*, section 5): setting up the real data in this
   repo (qPCR, dilutions, volumes) while further data are sourced and the
   eDNA model changes are made in HSGP4eDNA.

## 2. Where things stand

- **Models.** One latent field per species (2-D HSGP plus a bottom-depth
  spline, animals per km²) on the study domain north of 35°N (690 × 1770 km),
  with an 800-function basis budget.
  - **eDNA:** HSGP4eDNA v1.5 (qPCR hurdle for hake, zero-inflated
    beta-binomial metabarcoding with a junk category, between-bottle and junk
    noise).
  - **Visual:** `stan/hsgp_visual.stan` (half-normal or hazard-rate detection,
    group-size and Beaufort covariates, size-biased group sizes, Poisson
    segment counts).
  - **Joint:** `stan/hsgp_joint.stan`, both observation models on one field,
    with likelihood switches, optional free conversion factors, the Beaufort
    covariate per species, a data-based starting field and 4 chains.
- **Checks.** `tests/smoke.R` (31 checks), `tests/joint_exactness.R` (the
  joint model against the model before the Beaufort covariate and against the
  visual models), `tests/visual_detection.R`.
- **Tier-1 results so far** (README Results; explainer Section 5):
  - visual validation, 5 seeds plus 3 with sea state: every fit converged;
  - joint comparison, calm sea, seed 1: joint best for both cetaceans,
    conversion factors recovered;
  - joint comparison with sea state and the Beaufort covariate, seeds 1–2:
    the same pattern, Beaufort slopes covered.
- **HSGP4eDNA (a parallel session; its `ROADMAP.md`, from 2026-10-21).**
  Changes to the eDNA side that reach this repo at the next submodule bump:
  - **v1.6, data-based starting field** in its fit drivers (the
    `edna_field_start()` port; committed on a local branch, not yet merged);
  - **junk redrawn for each aliquot** in the simulators, instead of once per
    bottle. In the real data the share of a bottle's main junk taxon varies
    between its replicates a median 570× more than multinomial sampling
    alone gives;
  - **simulator options**: per-category amplification efficiency and PCR
    cycles, junk as many taxa, a dilution per metabarcoding replicate, and
    departures from the mechanism (clumped copies, PCR drift);
  - **v1.7, mechanistic metabarcoding overdispersion** behind a switch,
    ported from Shannon Kachel's bbqm: φ and the read shares follow from
    Poisson aliquot copies and amplification, instead of a fitted curve. It
    has a composite junk category with a free amplification efficiency, a
    global offset on log φ as the check on the mechanism, and data for
    amplification (`log_amp`) and a per-replicate dilution
    (`mb_log_dilution`). Because φ grows with total copies, the replicate
    spread then carries information on absolute copies, a second handle
    besides hake qPCR;
  - then its 35°N validation (with both φ forms) and a release.

  Notes for Shannon on bbqm: `notes/bbqm_notes_for_shannon.md`.
- **Compute.** The Mac runs about two 4-chain fits at once; the stats3 server
  (`notes/stats3_guide.md`, `tools/stats3_reruns.sh`) runs the larger batches.

## 3. Tier 1: finish the idealised simulation

In order.

1. **Negative-binomial segment counts** (*next*). The real visual fits show
   length-scales following their prior floor, which clustered sightings
   (counts more variable than Poisson) could cause.
   - First simulate clustered sightings (negative-binomial counts) and check
     whether the Poisson model reproduces the floor-following length-scales,
     and whether a negative-binomial term removes them.
   - Then add it to the visual and joint models: segment counts negative
     binomial around the current Poisson mean, with an overdispersion per
     species and a switch whose off position is exactly the Poisson model
     (checked as in `tests/joint_exactness.R`); a smoke-test case.
2. **Replicate comparison with sea state, seeds 1–5** (after item 1 and the
   HSGP4eDNA bump, item 3, so that they run once on the model that goes
   forward).
   `SEA_STATE=1 Rscript R/compare_joint.r` per seed and mode, or
   `CMP_SEA=1 tools/stats3_reruns.sh stage 3` on stats3. Seeds 1 and 2 were
   run before item 1 (explainer Section 5) and are rerun on the new model.
   - Fits with the Beaufort covariate for all five seeds; the
     without-covariate contrast (visual, joint, joint with free conversion
     factors) on a subset, to show what leaving it out costs.
   - Then a summary table across seeds (field R², interval width and
     coverage, β_bft, conversion factors, convergence) for the README and the
     explainer.
3. **Bump HSGP4eDNA** (v1.6, then v1.7; its roadmap §2.7).
   - **v1.6** gives `fit_edna()` and `R/validate_joint_sim.r` the data-based
     start (the joint-simulator seed 1 failure: at extreme hake hot spots a
     flat start left every chain at near-zero copies; ROADMAP_2026-10-06,
     6.0 item 5). Then rerun the joint-simulator check.
   - **The simulator default changes:** `simulate_joint()` calls
     `simulate_edna_obs()`, so junk is redrawn per aliquot after the bump.
     Decide whether to pass `junk_aliquot = "constant"` until the replicates
     are rerun, or rerun them on the redrawn junk (recommended: the
     replicates in item 2 have not run yet).
   - **v1.7:** pass `mb_phi_model`, `log_amp`, `mb_log_dilution` and the junk
     efficiency prior through `hsgp_joint.stan`, `format_stan_data_joint()`,
     `fit_joint()` (initial value for `log_w_junk`) and `compare_joint.r`;
     extend `tests/joint_exactness.R` (off position equals the previous
     submodule); a smoke-test case with the mechanistic φ on.
4. **HSGP4eDNA 2-D validation on the 35°N domain** (its roadmap §2.6: run once,
   after the junk redraw and the mechanistic φ). `nobathy_surface` × gp2d
   and `bathysp_surface`, `bathysp_depthspref`, `bathygp_surface` ×
   gp2d_bathysp (stats3 stage 2), giving a new HSGP4eDNA README §4 table and a
   release. The §2 basis sweep and the 3-D cells stay marked as on the
   old box.
5. **Joint-model predictive checks.** Prior and posterior predictive checks
   for the joint model, in the style of the explainer's Sections 3 and 4.
6. **Explainer regeneration.** The simulated eDNA and visual fits and figures
   (`docs/explainer/01`–`10`; stats3 stage 4), so that Sections 2–4 move to
   the 35°N set-up and the out-of-date note at the top goes.
7. **Optional scenarios in the joint model.** `bathysp_depthspref` (water
   column and depth preference) and a `bathygp_*` truth as a deliberate
   mis-specification check.

## 4. Tier 2: realistic simulation

The sampling is real; the truth is known but not generated by the model.
Details of the surfaces to follow.

- **Truth:** previously modelled density surfaces for PWSD, humpback and hake,
  put on the 35°N study domain (UTM 10N, km from the domain origin) in the
  model's units (animals per km²; hake's unit to decide).
- **Visual observations** simulated on the actual 2018 effort (1,641 segments
  north of 35°N, with their real lengths and Beaufort), using the real-data
  detection-function fits and the empirical group-size pools.
- **eDNA observations** simulated at the actual 2019 bottles (551, with their
  real locations, sampling depths and replicate structure), with conversion
  factors, volumes and noise from the idealised simulator or from the
  section 5 data, whichever exist.
- **Fits:** eDNA-only, visual-only, joint and joint with free conversion
  factors. The model is mis-specified here, so this tests robustness as well
  as the sampling design.
- **Measures:** recovery on a prediction grid over the whole domain, not only
  at sampled locations (R², error, 95% interval coverage, and how they degrade
  away from effort and stations); abundance by region; conversion factors;
  several replicate observation draws from the same truth.
- **Code needed:**
  - a simulator that takes truth surfaces and the real designs from
    `data/processed/` and returns the `simulate_joint()` object shape, so the
    formatters, `fit_joint()` and `compare_joint.r` apply unchanged;
  - prediction on a grid in the joint model (it has none yet; the visual
    model's `pred_coords` is the template);
  - a comparison script that scores the grid predictions.
- **Depends on:** section 3 item 1 (negative-binomial counts) if the real
  sightings are clustered. The Beaufort covariate is already in.

## 5. Tier 3: real data (in progress: data set-up)

The real-data set-up can go ahead in this repo while HSGP4eDNA's changes are
made; the likelihood terms it needs come from HSGP4eDNA (item 3).

1. **What we have for eDNA** (raw files private, in `data/`, git-ignored):
   - `qPCR_2019A.rds` (qPCR lab, "analysis A", received 2026-10-12), now the
     loader's qPCR source: for every hake well its **Ct**, plate, well,
     dilution (fraction), inhibition rate, `drop.sample` flag; per sample
     `depth_cat` (target depth), filtered volume, date; hake **standards** on
     all 33 plates; field negatives and controls. Cleaned by the lab: one
     dilution per sample, no inhibited wells, outliers dealt with.
   - `M3_compiled_taxon_table_wide.csv` (now the metabarcoding source): reads
     per taxon (rows) for 3,629 replicate columns over six markers; the
     loader keeps MarVer1 (`MV1`, 1,709 columns). Column names carry sample,
     dilution fold and replicate.
   - `Hake_2019_metadata.csv`: per sample, station, Niskin, lat/lon,
     recorded depth, volume, date, control flags, `drop.sample`.
   - The earlier `hake_qPCR_MURI_df.csv` and `MV1_MURI_df.csv` are no longer
     read.
   - The qPCR vignette's Stan models (`qPCR/` in the main checkout, not
     tracked); our standard-curve model `stan/qpcr_standards.stan` follows
     `qPCR_standards_multi-plate.stan`.
   - `docs/dataQs.md`: how qPCR and metabarcoding line up, the seven lab
     questions and Ole's answers (2026-10-21).
2. **Set-up in this repo** (items 1–8 done on `claude/real-data-plan`,
   2026-10-21; details in `notes/muri_data_formatting.md`, "eDNA from the lab
   files"):
   1. **qPCR from analysis A** — *done*. All analysis-A hake wells of the kept
      bottles: 1,532 wells on 25 plates, 3 per bottle for 458 of 513 bottles,
      detection rate 0.858. Bottles matched across files on sample number.
   2. **Standard curves** — *done*. `R/fit_qpcr_standards.r` fits
      `stan/qpcr_standards.stan` (per-plate intercept and slope; shared
      γ₀, γ₁, σ₀ and κ; vignette priors) to the 659 non-inhibited hake
      standards on 33 plates: no divergences, R̂ ≤ 1.003; `alpha_ct`
      36.1–39.9, `beta_ct` 1.45–1.49 (efficiency 0.95–1.00); κ 0.90;
      97% of detected standards inside their 95% predictive interval. Each
      well carries its plate's curve (`qpcr_alpha_ct`, `qpcr_beta_ct`);
      `truth$qpcr_params` holds the plate means and the shared terms. The
      10⁵ standards are all inhibited, so the curves rest on 1–10⁴ copies/µL.
   3. **Dilutions** — *done*. `qpcr_log_dilution` = log fraction (1 or 0.2);
      `mb_log_dilution` = −log fold. Where a bottle's metabarcoding was run
      at several folds (52 bottles), the fold with the most reads is kept.
   4. **Volumes** — *done* for filtered volume per bottle (`vol_filtered`:
      2.5 L for 511 bottles). qPCR template 2 µL (the vignette). The
      MarVer1 template volume is assumed to be the same 2 µL (asked, item 4);
      it matters because qPCR fixes absolute copies and metabarcoding
      detection depends on the copies in its own reaction. The elution
      volume (100 µL, assumed) is common to both assays and only rescales the
      conversion factor, so it is not needed unless we report that factor in
      physical units.
   5. **Dates** — *done* (2019-07-02 to 2019-08-17). `cruise` = project
      52193 (the 2019 hake survey; one survey, so nothing uses it).
   6. **Contamination** — *done*. *Gallus* (127,162 reads) is dropped from
      the junk and the totals; the M3 MarVer1 table has no human, cattle or
      pig reads.
   7. **Bottom depth** — *done*. Bottles use the metadata's
      `bottom.depth.consensus` (the lab's answer); ETOPO 2022 at the bottle is
      kept as `Z_bathy_etopo` (median absolute difference 13 m), and the
      visual segments keep ETOPO.
   8. **Checks** — *done*. `R/02_check_muri.r` passes on the new object and
      the HSGP4eDNA v1.5 formatter accepts it (N 513, 1,532 qPCR wells, 1,438
      metabarcoding replicates). The detection-function AIC selection on the
      35°N effort gives the same result as before: hazard-rate + group size
      for humpback; for PWSD half-normal with visibility (−6.8 AIC),
      Beaufort and group size, of which we use group size + Beaufort. That
      set is 9.7 AIC worse than the selected one and no better than no
      covariates (section 6).
   - Also in the object: `depth_cat` (target depth: 0, 50, 150, 300, 500 m)
     replaces recorded depth as `Z_sample` (recorded minus target: −36 to
     +3 m); 30EtOH samples (56 of 569, 10%) are dropped by default (`ETOH`
     env var); 7 bottles with no metabarcoding reads keep their qPCR.
3. **Needed from HSGP4eDNA** (to add to its roadmap; some are there already;
   the object already carries the fields named here):
   - a **qPCR dilution offset** per well (its §3; `qpcr_log_dilution`);
   - **filtered volume per bottle** (a vector instead of the single
     `log_vol_filtered`; `samples$vol_filtered`) — *new request*;
   - **standard curves estimated in the model** — *new request*, decided
     2026-10-21. The hake standards enter the eDNA model as data, and the
     qPCR observation parameters become parameters instead of fixed data:
     per-plate intercept and slope (`alpha_ct`, `beta_ct`, slope bounded at
     doubling per cycle), with the Ct-noise terms (γ₀, γ₁, σ₀) and κ shared
     across plates, as in the vignette's field-sample model and our
     stand-alone fit (`stan/qpcr_standards.stan`). Each field well needs
     its plate index (`qpcr_plate_idx`). Use all 33 plates' standards. The
     request text for HSGP4eDNA's roadmap is in
     `notes/hsgp4edna_standard_curves.md`;
   - the **metabarcoding dilution** offset (its §2.4, `mb_log_dilution`), for
     both φ forms;
   - optionally, the 30EtOH loss as a known or estimated offset (section 6);
   - a formatter that accepts replicate totals with dropped taxa removed (its
     §2.4);
   - bounding the Ct-noise SD at very low copies once the real standard curve
     is in (its §3; the other feature behind the seed 1 trap).
4. **Still needed from collaborators:** the follow-ups in `docs/dataQs.md`
   (hake in field negatives; a minimum
   total-read cutoff for a
   MarVer1 replicate to count as successful; the template volume per MarVer1
   PCR);
   MarVer1 mock-community data with known proportions for these species, and
   the PCR cycle number per library (and whether diluted and undiluted
   templates get the same cycles).
5. **Model work before a real fit:**
   - **Amplification efficiency.** The model assumes MarVer1 amplifies every
     species and the junk equally. With a fixed cycle number a species' bias
     shifts its surface but not its shape; for the cetaceans it biases
     eDNA-only absolute density and, in joint fits, is confounded with the
     conversion factor; if cycles or dilutions vary by sample it enters the
     spatial pattern. In HSGP4eDNA v1.7 it becomes `log_amp` in the
     mechanistic φ, with a mock-community prior.
   - **Is eDNA amount proportional to density?** Free an elasticity
     (copies ∝ λ^b), the test suggested by the CalCOFI comparison
     (`docs/calcofi_hake_comparison.md`), which found eDNA amount carried
     little information beyond presence for whales.
   - **Sampling depths:** a `depth_cat` effect (section 6).
   - **Survey years:** the 2018 visual and 2019 eDNA surveys are treated as
     one snapshot.
6. **Real eDNA fit, then the real joint fit**, with the predictive checks
   (section 3 item 5), then the explainer's real-data sections and the real
   eDNA explainer fit (`expl_edna_real`).
7. **Hake qPCR-only samples.** Analysis A has 1,771 hake field samples; 513
   are our metabarcoded bottles. The other 1,258 (3,797 wells; same survey,
   dates and latitudes) were **not metabarcoded** (confirmed by the lab,
   2026-10-21), so they will only ever add hake qPCR. Where they are:
   - 484 are a second bottle at a station × depth we already have;
   - 613 are other depths at our stations, including 305 at 100 m, a level
     missing from the metabarcoded set;
   - 161 are at 19 stations we do not have;
   - 197 are flagged 30EtOH (same decision as for the metabarcoded ones).

   What they would add: (1) **bottle-to-bottle noise**, the main gain:
   only 16 locations now have two bottles, so the noise is weakly separated
   from the spatial signal (the real fit's very large noise terms are one
   symptom), and if the noise is shared across species this helps the
   cetaceans too; (2) the **hake depth effect**: the 100 m level and more
   bottles at every depth, partly separating sampling depth from bottom
   depth; (3) about 3.5× the hake qPCR data and 19 new stations for the
   hake surface. They add nothing directly for humpback or PWSD, and
   nothing on how qPCR and metabarcoding relate. Cost: about 1,580 bottles
   instead of 513, roughly 3× the eDNA part of the fit (per-bottle noise
   terms); the model already takes bottles with qPCR only (7 now).

   Plan: fit the 513 metabarcoded bottles first, as the clean comparison;
   then add the qPCR-only bottles, starting with the 484 paired bottles and
   the 100 m samples, where the gain per bottle is largest, and compare.
   The loader needs an option to include them (an env var like `ETOH`).

## 6. Decisions pending

- **GP marginal-SD prior:** its 99% bound (3) is below the real visual fits
  (2.8–3.6). A new upper bound, e.g. 5?
- **qPCR** (tier 3). Settled with Ole's answers (2026-10-21): analysis A's
  wells as provided (one dilution per sample, late Ct kept), dilutions as
  offsets in both assays. Decided 2026-10-21: the standard curves are
  estimated jointly in the eDNA model from the standards (per-plate
  intercept and slope, shared noise terms and κ; section 5 item 3), not
  fixed or used as priors. The stand-alone fit stays as a check (the joint
  curves should match it), for starting values, and as the qPCR truth for
  tier 2. Still open:
  - **30EtOH** samples (56 of 569 metabarcoded, 10%; the mix-up lost about
    20–30% of hake DNA): dropped for now, or kept with a loss factor
    estimated from hake qPCR and shared by the other taxa;
  - **hake in field negatives**: 73 of 114 evaporated-DI and 25 of 72 lab-DI
    field-negative wells detect hake (up to about 12 copies/µL); extraction
    and PCR controls are clean. A contamination term, or a question for the
    lab;
  - adding the 1,258 hake qPCR-only samples: planned as a second step
    (section 5 item 7).
- **Sampling depth:** `depth_cat` as an ordered categorical with uneven
  spacing (0, 50, 150, 300, 500 m; no 100 m bottles). A per-species smooth
  or random walk over the levels, rather than five free effects, and watch
  its confounding with bottom depth.
- **PWSD detection covariates:** AIC selects visibility + Beaufort + group
  size; we use group size + Beaufort (no better than none). Use the AIC set,
  or none.
- **Contamination:** done for *Gallus*; the M3 MarVer1 table has no human or
  cattle reads.
- **Realistic simulation** (tier 2): which surfaces, and their source, years,
  season, resolution and units (hake: biomass or numbers); whether to rescale
  them to plausible eDNA detection rates; one static truth or a surface per
  survey year.
